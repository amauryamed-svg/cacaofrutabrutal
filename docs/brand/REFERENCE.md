## Brand Foundation

**Brand:** CAUA — Upcycled cacao foodtech for functional health and wellbeing
**Motto:** "With Nature We Walk" — statement and promise to our mission
**Focus:** Cacao as superfood, NOT chocolate or candy

### Mission
Promote wellbeing and nourishment, reduce waste, and inspire empowerment through cacao.

### Purpose
Reignite the cacao industry and create opportunities for restoration and community building.

### Goal
Elevate cacao as a powerhouse superfood and premium consumer experience while amplifying our core mission and purpose.

### Core Values
1. Environmental Conservation
2. Circular Economy
3. Biodiversity Preservation
4. Ethical Sourcing
5. Whole Ingredients
6. Nutrient Density
7. Transparency

### Point of View
- Treat nature and others with care and respect
- Reduce waste by creating efficiencies
- Give back to the land and communities that provide
- Freedom is a value before it's a condition
- Individual sovereignty begins with health
- Uplifting the impoverished and unprivileged is a responsibility

### Target Audience
**Conscious health seekers and foodies**
- Educated, Healthy, Social, Community/family oriented
- Ages 10+ (main 18–45)
- Lifestyle: Active/Athletic, Adventurous, Outgoing, Creative, Entrepreneurial

## Messaging Guidelines

### Voice & Tone

| Voice        | Tone           |
|--------------|----------------|
| Motivating   | Authenticity   |
| Inspiring    | Confidence     |
| Captivating  | Commitment     |
| Encouraging  | Respect        |

**How We Speak:**
We speak with care and compassion. With intention. Our words are uplifting and promising. We use words that motivate and inspire. You feel valued, welcomed, heard. You feel alive and great.

### Word Bank

**Adjectives:** Social, Exciting, Energizing, Regenerative, Refreshing, Revitalizing, Rejuvenating, Vibrant, Lively, Healthy, Nourishing, Beneficial, Cleansing, Crisp, Vibe, Caring

**Nouns:** Adventure, Connection, Community, Family, Humble, Positive, Cultured, Sacred, Educated, Valued, Future, Successful, Team

**Verbs:** Activate, Alchemize, Initiate, Explore, Cultivate, Imbue, Challenge, Inform, Preserve, Supersede

**Proper Nouns / Terms:** Functional Cacao, Upcycled Cacao, Functional Beverage, Beneficial Beverage, Bioflavonoids, Activation, Fermentation

### Critical Distinctions
- ✅ **Cacao** — never "chocolate"
- ✅ **Superfood** — never "candy"
- ✅ `caua` (all lowercase) or `CAUA` (all uppercase) — never mixed case like "Caua"
- ✅ Always emphasize the accent mark (brand mark)

### Copywriting Standards
AP style with occasional variations. Match tone to section:
- **Hero:** Bold promise + functional science ("Mucílago 20% · Epicatequina · Teobromina")
- **Product:** NAME IN UPPERCASE + functional descriptor lowercase
- **Guardian:** Region + Power + Heritage — data, not narrative
- **CTA:** Action verb + destination ("RESERVA TU LOTE", not "Haz clic aquí")

## Visual Identity

### Logo

**Design Philosophy:** Bold, Simple and Meaningful

**Key Attributes:**
- Minimalistic aesthetics
- Captivate health attributes and healthy culture
- Show respect to the planet/partners/ingredients
- Guilt-free enjoyment — for the body, world and workers

**Logo Hierarchy:**

| Level     | Description                                                             |
|-----------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Primary   | `caua` wordmark lowercase with distinctive accent mark in Cosmic Criollo purple |
| Secondary | `caua` + tagline "with nature we walk" (Mazorca Yellow)                 |
| Secondary | `caua` + cacao pod icon + tagline                                       |
| Tertiary  | Text around cacao pod icon                                              |
| Tertiary  | Standalone cacao pod icon                                               |
| Tertiary  | Jaguar/animal icon (biodiversity symbol)                                |

**Specifications:**
- **Minimum Size:** 1 inch / 75px
- **Proportion:** 1:4 ratio using the "A" as measurement unit
- **Clear Space:** Minimum = 50% scale of the "C" around entire logo on all sides
- **Cropping:** May be cropped for specific applications, must maintain legibility

**Logo Don'ts:**
- Do not alter, change, or distort the logo in any way not shown in guidelines
- Do not change colors outside approved variants
- Do not add drop shadows, outlines, or unapproved effects
- Do not place on low-contrast backgrounds

## Typography

**Personality:** Exciting, Functional and Lively

### Official Typefaces

| Role      | Font               | Weights                                         | Google Fonts Substitute    |
|-----------|--------------------|-------------------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Primary   | **Acumin Pro**     | Ultra Black, Black, Bold, Medium, Regular, XLight (+ italics) | **DM Sans** |
| Secondary | **Gelica**         | Black, Bold, Medium, Regular, Extra Light (+ italics) | **Cormorant Garamond** |
| Support   | **Century Gothic** | Bold, Regular (+ italics)                       | **Barlow Condensed**       |

### App Font Stack (`constants.ts → FONTS`)
```ts
FONTS.display = "'Barlow Condensed', Impact, sans-serif"        // Century Gothic substitute
FONTS.body    = "'DM Sans', system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif" // Acumin Pro substitute
FONTS.serif   = "'Cormorant Garamond', 'Playfair Display', Georgia, serif" // Gelica substitute
```

### Rules
- Display headers: `FONTS.display` weight 900, UPPERCASE, letterSpacing `-0.01em` to `0.15em`
- Body text: `FONTS.body` weight 400/500, lineHeight 1.6–1.75
- Editorial/poetic moments: `FONTS.serif` italic, color Mazorca Yellow
- Labels/badges: `FONTS.display` weight 700, UPPERCASE, letterSpacing `0.12em+`
- Minimum font size in production: 10px

### Typography Mood Sample
- **Revitalizing** → Amazon Green `#1C3B26`
- **Crisp** → Cosmic Criollo `#8D2679`
- **Social** → Pod Green `#91A63B`, italic
- **Vibe** → Mazorca Yellow `#F1A91E`
- **Nourishing** → Theobroma Orange `#DB5527`
- **Beneficial** → Radio Red `#8C201D`

- Cacao pods (closed, cross-section, open)
- Cacao beans and seeds
- Cacao flowers (pentámero — 5 petals)
- Cacao leaves (theobroma leaf shape)
- Colombia map / origin geography
- Farm / agriculture scenes
- Sun and nature elements
- Sustainability / circular economy
- Meditation / wellness
- Fitness / athletic / running
- Globe / earth / biodiversity
- Community / connection / people
- Molecular / science (bioflavonoids, fermentation)
- Heart / health / brain / cognitive
- Jaguar (biodiversity, tertiary brand icon)

### Icon Rendering Rules
- **Stroke weight:** 0.4–1px botanical / 1–1.5px UI icons
- **Fill opacity:** max 0.35 (no solid fills on illustrations)
- **Color:** Pod Green primary; element colors for tarot/ritual contexts
- **Style:** Organic, slightly irregular — never perfectly geometric
- **No emoji as primary iconography** — use SVG botanical icons

## Design Principles

### Visual Aesthetic
- **Minimalistic** — Clean, uncluttered, generous whitespace
- **Natural** — Organic shapes, earth-inspired
- **Energizing** — Vibrant colors, dynamic compositions
- **Authentic** — Real imagery, genuine feel
- **Healthy** — Fresh, clean, wholesome

### Layout
- Generous white space
- Bold typography hierarchy: display → body → caption
- Organic shapes as design elements
- Clean information architecture

### Motion
- Hover: `0.3s ease`
- Hero entrance: `1.2–1.4s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` — translateY(48px→0) + opacity
- Float: `4–5s ease-in-out infinite` — translateY 0 → -8px → 0
- Glow pulse: box-shadow Pod Green cycling 0.2→0.5 opacity
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Anti-Patterns (PROHIBIDOS)

| ❌ Prohibido                                | ✅ Correcto                                 |
|--------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| "chocolate" / "candy"                      | "cacao" / "functional cacao" / "superfood"  |
| "Caua" (mixed case)                        | `caua` o `CAUA`                             |
| Gradientes pastel                          | Colores con peso real — brutalist luxury    |
| `var(--color)` en backgrounds              | Solo hex directos: `#040C06`                |
| `localStorage`                             | Supabase o React context                    |
| Demografía asumida en copy                 | Lenguaje neutro e inclusivo                 |
| Emoji como iconografía principal           | SVG botánicos line-based                   |
| Sombras grises neutras                     | Sombras con tinte de color de marca         |
| Bordes sólidos `#1C3B26`                  | Con alpha: `#1C3B2655`                      |
| "innovador" / "revolucionario"             | Sustancia real: datos, origen, proceso      |
| Stripe secret key en frontend              | Solo Supabase Edge Functions                |
